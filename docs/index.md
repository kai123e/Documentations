# Projects Home

This site documents projects I have worked on or am currently working on. It includes only projects that are suitable for publication on the internet and that serve an educational purpose — guides, tutorials, reproducible how‑tos, and reference notes intended to help others learn and reproduce the work.

## What you'll find here
- **Publishable projects:** Each entry is intentionally shareable online and avoids private or proprietary content.
- **Educational focus:** Projects are selected or written to teach a skill, demonstrate a technique, or provide a clear, repeatable setup.
- **Reproducible instructions:** Where applicable, I include step‑by‑step instructions, code, configuration and troubleshooting notes.

## Projects (summary)

### Amateur Radio
- Description: Guides and theory for amateur radio setups, including software like Direwolf and BBS/AX, plus practical setup notes.

### Arduino
- Description: Sensor and hardware projects — DS18B20 temperature sensors, HC‑05 Bluetooth modules, serial communication examples, and other microcontroller projects.

### Tiny House
- Description: Design notes and systems integration for tiny‑house projects, covering layout, utilities and efficiency considerations.

### Victron
- Description: Notes and demos around Victron energy systems, including Python authentication and integration examples.
- Educational goal: Show how to monitor and automate off‑grid energy systems.
